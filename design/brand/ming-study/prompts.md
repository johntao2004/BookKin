## App icon

This is a full-bleed, opaque Apple app-icon SOURCE MASTER, not a mockup, not an icon on a background canvas. Do NOT bake in rounded outer corners or an external drop shadow; the operating system will mask the square later. Design a singular original emblem: an ivory open thread-bound book whose upper silhouette subtly becomes a simple Ming-style geometric lattice window / pitched library roof. The icon must immediately read as BOOK + CHINESE SCHOLAR'S LIBRARY, with a strong simple silhouette recognizable at small sizes. Deep ink-teal celadon field, warm rice-paper ivory book, a tiny restrained cinnabar seal-like square accent with NO glyphs. Elegant shallow carved-relief depth, extremely fine handmade paper/ink texture, soft directional highlights. A quiet premium contemporary product icon informed by Ming woodblock book design, not ornamental imperial palace art. Center the large emblem within generous but not oversized internal safe area, fill most of the square with the colored field. No lettering, no Chinese characters, no English text, no watermark, no decorative tiny objects, no dragons, no Qing motifs, no Japanese torii, no pagodas. No existing brand marks, no Apple logo. One standalone icon only.
